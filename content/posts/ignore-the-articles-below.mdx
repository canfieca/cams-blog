I've always been a big fan of writing so a blog has always been something I toyed around with. So I finally decided to commit and make one. After writing my first post about illegal immigration I've decided to stick with politics.

Politics has become so ugly these days and people are unable to have a conversation in good faith. Most people just want to score points. So much so that I'm just done with having political conversations. People seldom change their minds, personal attacks often get invoked, and people don't actually want to listen.

So I decided this could be a good outlet to shut down political conversations just by saying if you want to know my opinion check out my blog.

So this doesn't become an echo chamber I am willing to make this a multi-user platform so people who disagree with my viewpoints can have an outlet to express that. If you think that's something you'd be interested in contributing to please let me know. If I have enough interest I'll do it but in order to add that functionality it would take a lot of work on my end. So I don't want to do if people aren't actually going to contribute.

You don't necessarily need to have a desire to disagree with me in order to use this platform you can post your opinions as well if you're looking for the same benefit I have.
