Golf is a game I love and enjoy, does it love me back? Not always. My drives are hit-or-miss, my iron shots have been getting better, my chips on to the green have always been abysmal, my putts however - always solid.

## My Drive

Something weird about the driver for me is I don't like the light aspect of it. I love hitting my irons because it's a solid chunk of metal that's pretty easy to control the club face. The driver is so light that it feels like a precision swing as opposed to an iron swing which just feels more athletic.

That's why it's probably the worst part of my game, is because I approach things from an athletic muscle memory stand point. Any deviation from a perfect strike will send your ball anywhere but straight. The fact that it's tee'd up also introduces more room for error. Tee it too low you'll hit a worm burner and hit it too high, to the sky!

I have a hard time controling the club face upon impact I really do it's hard. It's so light that any small amount of torque of the wrists will over adjust the driver head. I'd like to think I'm a fairly strong guy with high amounts of muscle mass so I always try to use my muscles in the swing and it's just not that kind of a swing.

My cousin Connor can consistently out drive me, but what I will say if you're reading this Connor, my longest drive is longer than your longest so suck my nuts. The reason I mention this though is because he's smaller than me, but the reason he can consistently out drive me is because he has better control of the club face upon impact. The reason I also mention that my longest drive his longer than his because it would show that when I hit it great, my muscles are then allowed to surpass his muscle mass.

A thing that has been helping a lot on the course is say I know I can consistently hit my driver 250 yards I look down the fairway to a point I guess is 250 yards away. I will then subtract 20 yards from that point back towards me and aim for that spot. What this does is allow me to approach the shot from a perspective of I'm not trying to hit it as far as I can I'm trying to shorten my shot. I then swing nice and easy but the funny thing that ends up happening is the ball will actually carry the point that was 250 yards away even though in my mind I was aimed shorter.

## My Irons

I brushed upon this in the Driver section but the iron swing is much more of an athletic swing in my opinion. I just played yesterday and I tee'd up a 6 iron for a par 3 (160 yards). I've been getting so much better that I chose my 6 iron because I know I can hit it 160 yards fairly easy but ended up blowing past the green by like 10 - 15 yards. So now I need to readjust all my numbers.

The weighted club head just feels easier to control for me than the driver I also have custom fitted irons with a heavier shaft weight than average. The heavier the club is for me the easier time I have swinging it.

So in terms of swing I think I'm satisfied with my iron swing, but the thing that messes up my iron swing is my lie. Sometimes I just put myself in shit grass because of my aforementioned inability to hit a straight drive. 

Now it's true that any golfer would have a bad swing in bad conditions and most of my bad iron swings are because of bad conditions. The reason why I count this as a part of my game that could be better is because I am the reason why most of iron shots are in bad conditions.

## My Chips 

My chips are pretty bad for the most part once again for the same reason, bad lie. Also because I don't have a lob wedge, I used to but then I got the custom fitted new set that came with either a sand wedge or a gap wedge and I chose sand.

I went through a little phase where I put the old lob and approach wedge back in my bag but found myself in this weird yo-yoing between my custom fitted swing and a regular swing. So I just took them out for consistency purposes and as of two games ago am now trying to use my send wedge on the grass for chip shots. 

I hit a beautiful chip shot with my sand wedge at Bethpage Red that left myself 3 feet from the hole for par. The people I was playing with told me I could pick up but no I had to hear it hit the bottom of the cup.

## My Putts

Put me anywhere on the green and I'm two putting it, I'm sorry, I am. Something about my putting game has always been great. I give myself about a 95% accuracy from 6 feet and about 75% from 7 feet. The reason I always two putt is because all I need to do is put the ball within 7 feet of the cup and I'm sinking it.

This makes it drastically easier to two putt from anywhere on the green. Say if I start off from 40-50 feet away, trying to put the ball within 6 feet of the hole is a lot easier than trying to sink it. So that's all I need to do I'm the best lag putter out of everyone I play with and I will die on this hill.

Please hit my line on some bullshit like you're a better putter than me, you're not. I'm sorry it's just the truth. You can be a better driver than me I don't give a fuck, drive for show putt for dough. Only reason why Bryson has a US Open victory is because Rory choked, not once, but TWICE from FIVE FEET. ON HOLE 18. 

So don't ever hit my line on some bullshit saying you're a better golfer than me cause I have beaten people on many holes where they had a great drive, I had a shit drive. They had a great iron onto the green, I had a shit iron that I chunked and left myself short of the green, that I once again chunk it somewhere close to the hole. Now what, they're putting for a birdie and I'm putting for a par? What does the great driver do? He fucking three putts and finishes with a bogey. What do I do? Fucking sink it! Everytime for par! Could you imagine how great of a golfer I would be if I had a great long and short game? Unstoppable and unbeatable. 

In fact most people don't beat me by a large margin, unless I'm just having a shitty day which every golfer does? I tie most holes on 18 just because I two putt and they three putt.

## My Mental Game

My mental game could use improvement. As a former D1 collegiate rower, I'm used to enduring intense physical activity with have having a lot of thoughts racing through my head. That type of physical activity calls for a strong mental game in resillence and perserverance. 

How do I make use of this as a golfer now? It's actually a huge detriment to my mental game because as a golfer your mental game must be silence and precision. I'm used to doing something physical with thoughts going through my head but when I take a golf swing with thoughts going through my head I fuck up the shot.

> Easy back swing, strong down swing, keep the club face square with the ball when I hit this shit, make sure I swing up so it launches into the sky...

Yeah all that shit definitely fucks up my shot. You want to know what's going through my head when I hit my best shots? Absolutely nothing. But golf is a game of inches most importantly the six inches between your ears. I guess looking into methods of how to improve my golf mental game will be helpful.

## Final Thoughts on how I play Golf

I played a lot of golf when I was a kid because I loved golfing and played just to play. But then I got to middle school and I stopped playing and then never really returned to it until my senior year of college.

So that was literally a decade long break I took from it and you may be asking yourself why did I get back into it in college. I'll be completely honest, for the sole purpose of drinking and getting drunk with my friends.

We would always play scrambles so if I hit a shit shot my partner would normally hit a better one then I carried us on the green. Then I came back from college back to my family after I was telling them I play so much golf now, which I did. I actually played the most golf as a senior in college than I did any other time in my life in terms of regularlity. I played once, twice, sometimes even three times a week.

But the only way to get better at playing golf is the fact you have to practice it more than you play it. I was playing it more than I practiced it. The reason people like my cousin has better control of the club face upon impact is because he loves going to the range every day.

I have a love hate relationship with driving ranges and golf practice specifically. To get better at basketball you can by a hoop and ball for your backyard to get better at. Football same thing you just need to by a football and you can toss it around in your backyard.

What I hate about practicing golf at the range is the fact that it's sort of a fucking chore if I'm being honest. You have to get your bags in your car, drive 20 - 30 minutes away to the range, spend an hour or two hitting balls down the range, maybe roll some putts for 20 minutes, then drive 20 - 30 minutes back home. When it's all said and done you've spent so much time practicing a hobby of yours that you're not getting paid to be good at by the way.

Then I always think about how much more productive I could've been in that time frame which is why I don't really enjoy going to the range. But in almost a round about way of finishing this article is to answer the question of it, why my game sucks? It's because I don't spend as much time at the range as I need to.
